# Beta test plan

## Purpose

Evaluate whether Loggr meets its functional/non-functional requirements and whether beginner, experienced, and supervising users can complete field tasks accurately and quickly.

## Participants

Recruit at least three intended users with informed teacher/client supervision:

1. An experienced amateur-radio operator or activator.
2. A beginner/youth participant using a non-identifying alias.
3. A Scout/club leader supervising multi-operator logging.

Record why each participant represents an intended user. Do not collect a youth participant’s full name, date of birth, email, or other identifying details.

## Scenarios

1. Start a POTA activation and choose the appropriate mode/role.
2. Log three valid contacts, including one P2P contact and one duplicate attempt.
3. Correct an entry, delete an unwanted entry, and switch operator.
4. Briefly disconnect the network, log another contact, reconnect, and verify recovery/sync status.
5. End the session, interpret the report, export ADIF, and (with an authorised operator) validate it in the intended portal.

## Data collection

- Observation checklist: completion, errors, assistance required, and task time.
- Short post-test questionnaire: 1–5 ratings for clarity, speed, confidence, outdoor readability, and overall usefulness.
- Semi-structured interview: what was confusing, what should change, and which mode they would use.

Using three methods provides behavioural, quantitative, and qualitative evidence. Store responses by anonymous participant code.

## Acceptance targets

- At least 90% of scenario steps completed without critical error.
- Median normal-mode contact entry under 10 seconds after familiarisation.
- All locally entered contacts survive an offline/reload test.
- No invalid ADIF record in the tested export.
- Median clarity and confidence ratings of at least 4/5.
